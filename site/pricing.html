Evaluate before you buy

You may use Blacksite commercially for 30 days without a license, so you can find out whether it earns a place in your workflow before anyone has to raise a purchase order. After that, continued commercial use needs a license.

Do I need a commercial license?

You do if you use Blacksite in the course of work for a for-profit company, on commercial products, on client work, or in any activity with an anticipated commercial application. Company size doesn't change that, and neither does whether the specific code you point it at earns revenue directly.

You don't for personal projects, learning, experimentation, or work at a qualifying noncommercial organization.

The license text is the authority on where exactly the line falls. If your situation is close to it, read the license — or just ask.

Model costs are separate

Blacksite is bring-your-own-key. You connect your own Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or AWS Bedrock account and that provider bills you for tokens directly. A Blacksite license covers the tool, not inference — and no request is ever proxied through a server of ours, because there isn't one.

Getting a license

Get in touch with your company name, roughly how many developers would use it, and anything unusual about your environment.
