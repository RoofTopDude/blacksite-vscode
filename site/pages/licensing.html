{{icon:info}} Common questions

The parts people
ask about.

Short answers. The license text governs in every case where these differ from it.

Does a fork, clone, or download give me commercial rights?
No. A GitHub fork, clone, download, package installation, marketplace installation, contribution, or evaluation does not create a commercial license. Commercial rights exist only under a written license issued or accepted by the licensor.
My company is a startup with no revenue yet. Do we need a license?
Yes, if the use has an anticipated commercial application — which building a product you intend to sell does. Company size and current revenue do not change the analysis. The 30-day evaluation exists so you can try it first.
Does Blacksite claim any rights over the code I write with it?
No. You own the code and materials you create with assistance from the software, subject to any rights of the model providers or other third-party services you connect. The licensor does not claim ownership of your project code merely because Blacksite was used to help create it.
What counts as an Automation Seat?
One concurrently operating non-human execution environment: a service account, CI runner, background agent, virtual machine, or container that uses or controls the software. It is a concurrency metric, not a total. An order form can define a different metric where that fits your environment better.
Can contractors use our seats?
Yes, while performing services for you, under written confidentiality and use restrictions at least as protective as the agreement, and within your purchased seat count. You remain responsible for their compliance.
Can we use it on client work?
Yes — you may develop, test, maintain, review, and deliver work product for your clients. What you may not do without an explicit grant is transfer, sublicense, host, or otherwise make the software itself available to a client. Client personnel who use it directly need to be covered by your seats or their own license.
Air-gapped or regulated environment?
Welcome, and worth mentioning up front. Blacksite has no server component and collects no telemetry, which removes most of the usual friction, but license administration and update delivery need to be arranged deliberately in a disconnected environment.
Do you offer IP indemnification?
Only if expressly stated in an order form, subject to the scope, exclusions, and liability cap stated there. Ask if you need it; it is a negotiable term rather than a default one.
