{{icon:circle-help}} The boundary

Do I need a
commercial license?

The license text controls. This is the short, plain-language route to the answer; if your situation is close to the line, ask and we will help you place it.

{{icon:briefcase-business}} Talk to us if Blacksite is used…

  • By or for a for-profit company
  • In paid employment or contracting work
  • On a commercial product or service
  • For client work or internal business operations
  • In CI, background automation, or unattended business workflows
  • For work with an expected commercial application

{{icon:circle-check-big}} Generally free for…

  • Personal study and experimentation without an expected commercial application
  • Hobby projects
  • Qualifying charitable and educational organizations
  • Public research, safety, health, and environmental organizations
  • Government institutions

Commercial use can require a license even when Blacksite is only an internal tool and is not included in the product you ship. Company size and current revenue do not change that boundary.

{{icon:clock}} Want to evaluate it first?

Get in touch. We can arrange a nonproduction commercial evaluation before you commit to a paid license, and make the scope and timing clear in writing.

{{icon:globe}} Model costs are separate

Blacksite is bring-your-own-key. Your chosen model provider bills you directly for its API use; Blacksite does not proxy those requests.

{{icon:sliders-horizontal}} No one-size-fits-all metric

People, concurrent automation, a site-wide arrangement, or another practical measure can all be discussed. The written order form is where the agreed scope belongs.
