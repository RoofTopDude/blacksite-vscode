Blacksite is released under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md), which permits use for any **noncommercial purpose** — personal projects, research, study, hobby work, and use by charitable, educational, research, public safety, environmental, and government organizations.

Any other use requires a commercial license.

## Do I need one?

You need a commercial license if you use Blacksite in the course of work for a for-profit company, on commercial products, on client work, or in any activity with an anticipated commercial application. This applies regardless of company size or whether Blacksite is used directly on revenue-generating code.

You do **not** need one for personal projects, learning, experimentation, or work at a qualifying noncommercial organization. The license text is the authority on the exact boundaries — read it if your situation is close to the line.

## Evaluation

You may use Blacksite for commercial evaluation purposes for **30 days** without a commercial license, so you can determine whether it's worth buying before you buy it. After 30 days, continued commercial use requires a license.
